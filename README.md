# Domestique Manager
A cycling management game inspired by **Retro Bowl** and **Retro Goal**.


## 🚴 About the project
Earlier in the summer, right after the Tour de France, I started wondering:

> What would a cycling management game look like if it had the simplicity, accessibility and style of Retro Bowl and Retro Goal?

There are already great cycling management games, especially Pro Cycling Manager, but I was looking for something different. I wanted something simpler and more focused on the decisions, strategy and progression of managing a professional cycling team, while still letting you experience the races.

So I decided to try building it myself.

The small problem: **I have zero coding experience.**

I used AI to help me develop the game and spent about a month experimenting with the concept, mechanics, UI and gameplay.

Eventually, I stopped working on it because I couldn't get it as far as I wanted. However, I was quite happy with what I managed to create and thought the idea might be interesting enough to share.

I'm putting the project online in the hope that other cycling fans and developers might find it interesting and potentially take the idea further.


## 🎮 The prototype
The current version is a very early prototype.

It experiments with ideas such as:

- Managing a professional cycling team
- Rider management
- Rider attributes and specialities
- Team composition
- Contracts and finances
- Training and development
- Race strategy
- Different types of riders
- Breakaways and peloton dynamics
- Stage/race outcomes
- Season progression
- Team and rider progression
- A retro-inspired 8-bit visual style

A lot of these systems are still very basic or incomplete.

The goal was not to create a finished game, but to explore what a simplified cycling management game could feel like.


## 💻 The code
The code was largely created with the help of AI, as I don't have a programming background.

It is therefore probably not particularly elegant or well structured. 😅

If you're an experienced developer, don't judge me too harshly.

More importantly, feel free to experiment with it.


## 🛠️ Current state
This is **very much a prototype**.

There are bugs, unfinished systems, placeholder elements and plenty of things that could be improved.

Some mechanics were created simply to test an idea rather than as part of a fully designed system.

There is also a significant gap between what I imagined the game could become and what this prototype currently is.

That's kind of the point of sharing it.


## 💡 What I would like this to become
My original idea was to create something between a traditional sports management game and a simplified arcade game.

Something where you could:

**Build a team → Sign riders → Develop them → Plan your season → Make tactical decisions → Race → Earn money/reputation → Improve your team → Repeat**

The management side should be deep enough to create meaningful decisions, but simple enough that someone who knows nothing about professional cycling could pick it up and understand it.

The racing itself should also be an important part of the experience rather than simply being a simulation screen.
